Not another cruise app

We only show the top of the ship.

The mass-market booking sites bury the good cabins under a thousand interior fares. Sail Suitely does the opposite — it starts from the suite enclaves and all-inclusive luxury lines, and treats everything else as noise.

Browse by suite experience, butler service, all-inclusive, adults-only, or destination — the filters a luxury cruiser reaches for first.

A closer look

What it does.

Curated by prestige

The North Star Guide.

Every line placed by pedigree — ultra-luxury to premium — as celestial collections instead of an alphabetical list. See the fleet the way an insider does, and dive into the tier that fits how you sail.

Real, current fares

The real price — by cabin grade.

Every sailing shows honest, recently-refreshed "from" fares broken out by grade — suite, balcony, interior — always an indicative lead-in, never a fake quote. Then see how that fare has moved over time in a built-in price-history chart.

Price-drop alerts

Save it — we'll watch the price.

Favorite the sailings you're weighing and each becomes a card with everything that matters: the enclave, ship, itinerary basis and fares. With Sail Suitely Pro, track those fares and get alerted the instant one drops — like the gold "Price dropped 14%" pill here.

Compare

Two sailings, side by side.

Torn between two? Lay them out on one screen — suite & cabin fares, nights, region, both itineraries on the map, butler, all-inclusive. It even flags when a per-cabin and a per-person price aren't directly comparable, so you're never misled.

See where you'll sail

Every voyage on the map.

Each itinerary is plotted port-by-port on a real map, so you can picture the whole route at a glance — from a week in the Caribbean to a grand Pacific crossing.

Book direct

The whole itinerary — then book on the line's own site.

See the suite perks and the full port-by-port itinerary, then tap Book Now to go straight to the cruise line's own page for that exact voyage. No middle-man checkout, no markup.
